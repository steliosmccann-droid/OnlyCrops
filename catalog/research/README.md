# Only Crops — Historical FarmVille additions (2009–2020)

## Purpose

Research index of missing items from the original FarmVille; this **does not** add playable objects or change Market, grid, saves, sprite engine, or gameplay. The game remains CP20.7.

## Batch 01 — 43 historically named entries

- 19 crops/seeds not present in the game's four live seeds
- 15 trees absent from the existing object definitions
- 2 animal variants: Brown Cow and Black Sheep
- 7 functional/crafting buildings, mainly from 2010–2011

Data: [Batch 01 JSON](farmville-2009-2020-missing-batch-01.json). Existing IDs in the game are not overwritten.

## Admission rules

1. Historical name + source is enough for **research catalog** listing.
2. Do **not** add to live Market until precise unique ID mapping, grid footprint, currency, price, harvest mechanics and sprite are reviewed.
3. Preserve the approved seven top-level categories and existing save identifiers.
4. Do not invent unknown dimensions, prices, year or harvest returns. A null field means **unverified**.
5. Later batches must include 2012–2020 event/location-specific content, marked by the year and original farm/edition. Do not claim exhaustive 2009–2020 coverage yet.
6. Keep seasonal decorations, farm expansions, consumables and farmer clothing distinct from placeable objects; map them to an approved category or a non-placeable auxiliary catalog only after review.
